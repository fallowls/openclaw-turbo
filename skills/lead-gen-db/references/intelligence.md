# Intelligence Layer (Phase 5)

## Expansion Signals
- New India office announcements
- Hiring for India roles (Country Manager / Site Lead / GM India)
- Press releases mentioning India expansion

## Scoring (simple)
- +2 if hiring India leadership role
- +1 if company opened India office
- +1 if company funding in last 18 months

## Tags
Store tags in `lists` or `field_sources` JSON:
- expansion_signal: true
- india_intent_score: <int>
